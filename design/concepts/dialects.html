One world, eight dialects

The rooms feel distinct and purposeful — a constellation here, a library there, a bank, a food court — while the colours, the type and the controls stay one station. You can walk in cold and still guess what happens in a room on your first day.

1Familiar job, invented dialect. The arrangement changes; the job never does.

2One palette, one floor. Same tokens, same type ramp, same 44 px targets, same labels everywhere.

3The dialect never hides the job. Name and plain-job line in the same place, in every room, always — never colour alone.

4One place. Same stars outside every window, same lamplight, same deck underfoot — the rooms differ in what they hold, not in where they are.

5Alive, and ready at any hour. Someone is always on shift, every room answers a glance in ten seconds, and your assistant is one step away — whether you came for a list, a problem, or something new.

6Same hand, every room. One voice, one rhythm, one warmth — the room changes, the feel never does. Moving between rooms must never cause whiplash.

And underneath, all eight share this

One accent per room, chosen from one set — plus the same text, line and surface colours, the same type ramp, the same hit-area floor. A room may not add a colour or a gesture the others do not have.

Themes are a layer, not a rewrite. The eight dialects re-skin together — Navy & gold today, paper or night tomorrow — and no theme may lower the floor: same 44 px targets, same labels, same contrast, motion still yours to switch off.

What it should feel like

Calm, cared-for, curious, unhurried — a well-kept place where someone is home. Competent without being cold, alive without being loud, deep without being heavy. You should want to stay a while, and never feel behind.

Identify before reading. Colour, line, light, silhouette — all four, every room. Cover the name; you still know where you are.

Open it right up. A glance is enough, and the guts are always one step further in — inside the room, never a separate mode.

Always one more thing. What's new, what's mending, one thread found — bounded and opt-in. Never a firehose, never a red badge.

Ask, or move, from anywhere. Your assistant is always at hand; no room is a dead end, and no place is ever lost.
