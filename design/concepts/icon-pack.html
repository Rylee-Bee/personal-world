What it is about

A small pack of icons for what a person needs to know

thirteen marks

An alert says what it is about. Not whose it is, and not “Renai is doing a thing again” — the icon answers what am I being told, and what do I do with it? The name of whoever is involved belongs in the sentence, not on the button.
