Comfort & access

How this feels

Everything here is a comfort layer above the accessibility floor — it can never go below it. Changes apply everywhere and are remembered on this device. ✦

How it looks

The station's atmosphere. Colours, spacing, and motion — all above the accessibility floor.

Space between things
each theme changes the atmosphere, never the meaning
Let the map set the mood
entering a region tints the shell to it · atmosphere only, never meaning
Mermaid says:
"The way it looks affects how it feels. If the space between things is too tight, your eyes get tired. If it is too loose, you lose the thread. Try both and see which one your body prefers."

How it moves

Motion is opt-in. Your OS reduced-motion preference always wins.

Gentle motion
planet drift, breathing rings, companion visits · your OS reduced-motion always wins
Companions around
the companion orb that travels with you stays either way
Ratatoskr says:
"Motion can be delightful or it can be exhausting. There is no wrong answer. If you are not sure, leave it off — you can always turn it on later when you have more energy."

How much it asks

Low-demand keeps your world intact but lowers what it asks of you. Also available under "Help & quiet mode" in the topbar.

Ask less of me
keeps your world intact, lowers what it asks · also under "Help & quiet mode"
Robot says:
"Low-demand mode shows only what needs you. Everything else stays where it is. It is not hiding your world — it is just lowering the volume. You can bring it back any time."

What the server knows

Your data

Everything is stored on this device. Nothing leaves unless you say so. Clear individual sections or start fresh. These clear what is saved in your browser; the journal kept on the server is managed in the journal itself, not here.

Burrito Journalism says:
"Clearing data is permanent — there is no undo. But your world is yours to reset whenever you want. Sometimes a fresh start is exactly what you need."

Your companion

Pick who travels with you. Each companion has their own personality and templates.
