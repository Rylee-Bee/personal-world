# Project Worlds — Perfect UI Question Packet

100 questions. Answer what you can. Skip what you don't care about.
"Your call" means I'll use my judgment. "Don't care" means I won't
ask again.

---

## Surface & atmosphere (1–12)

**1.** Background: flat dark canvas, subtle gradient, or texture?
How dark? Pure #0a0810 or lighter?

**2.** Cards/panels: bordered, shadowed, glass/frosted, or flat?
How much contrast between card and canvas?

**3.** Should different screens feel like different rooms (different
surface treatments), or one consistent space?

**4.** How much whitespace is "enough"? Should content breathe like
a magazine or pack like a dashboard?

**5.** Should there be any ambient warmth effects (subtle radial
gradients, glow behind companion, etc.) or purely clean surfaces?

**6.** Dividers between sections: hard lines, soft fades, or just
spacing?

**7.** Corner radius: soft and round (12–16px), sharp (4–6px), or
mixed (sharp cards, round buttons)?

**8.** Should the sidebar/rail have a distinct surface (different
bg color) or blend with the canvas?

**9.** Any surfaces that should feel "special" — elevated, glowing,
highlighted — beyond normal cards?

**10.** The companion area: should it have its own ambient treatment
(glow, gradient, soft background) or just sit on the surface?

**11.** Loading states: skeletons, static text, spinner, or just
nothing until data arrives?

**12.** Empty states: illustration + text, just text, or minimal
icon + text?

## Typography (13–24)

**13.** Primary heading size for screen titles (h1): how big?
2rem? 2.5rem? Bigger?

**14.** Section heading size (h2): what feels right for card titles
and section labels?

**15.** Body text size: 14px? 15px? 16px?

**16.** Small/meta text size: 12px? 13px?

**17.** Line height for body text: tight (1.3), normal (1.5), or
generous (1.7)?

**18.** Should section titles be uppercase, sentence case, or mixed?

**19.** How much should Young Serif appear? Every h1? Only on Today
and World? Or more sparingly?

**20.** Monospace for technical details (commit hashes, etc.) — what
font, what size?

**21.** Text color hierarchy: how many levels? (primary, secondary,
muted — or more?)

**22.** Should the greeting on Today be notably larger than other
screen titles, or consistent?

**23.** Long text (journal entries, chat messages): what max-width
for comfortable reading? 40rem? 50rem? 60rem?

**24.** Text selection color: accent teal, or something else?

## Navigation (25–36)

**25.** Sidebar width on desktop: 200px? 240px? 280px? Should it
vary by screen?

**26.** Should the sidebar be collapsible? If so, to what — icon
rail or completely hidden?

**27.** Navigation link style: icon + label horizontal? Icon over
label vertical? Text only?

**28.** Active nav indicator: background highlight, left border,
underline, accent color, or bold text?

**29.** Places popover: how wide? Should it show icons for each
destination? Group labels?

**30.** Mobile bottom bar: 4 icons + labels? Should the active one
be accent-colored?

**31.** Should the "Project Worlds" brand/name appear in the sidebar?
If so, how prominent?

**32.** Companion in the sidebar: size, position (top? bottom?),
interactive or decorative?

**33.** Should there be any persistent search or command palette
access in the nav?

**34.** Notification badge/indicator on nav items: yes/no? If yes,
what style?

**35.** Should the sidebar show the current screen name somewhere,
or is the nav link enough?

**36.** How should "back" navigation work when you're deep in a
sub-view?

## Cards & data surfaces (37–48)

**37.** Card padding: tight (12px), normal (16–20px), or generous
(24–32px)?

**38.** Card header style: just title, or title + icon + count
badge?

**39.** Should cards have hover states? If so, what changes?

**40.** Card grid: how many columns max? Should cards have a min
width before stacking?

**41.** Status chips: pill shape? Border only? Background fill?
What sizes?

**42.** List items inside cards: separated by lines, spacing only,
or alternating backgrounds?

**43.** Should data tables exist anywhere in the product? If so,
what style?

**44.** Progress/status indicators: dots, bars, percentage, text
only, or stars?

**45.** "Quiet" cards (agent activity, low-priority info): dimmed
opacity, smaller text, or different border?

**46.** Card actions (links, buttons): inline at bottom of card,
in card header, or on hover?

**47.** Should cards be sortable/filterable, or always show the
system's default order?

**48.** How should "no data" appear inside a card that exists but
is empty?

## Buttons & controls (49–58)

**49.** Primary button style: filled accent? What border radius?
What text color?

**50.** Secondary button style: outlined? Ghost? What differentiates
it from primary?

**51.** Destructive action button: red fill? Red outline? Red text
on neutral?

**52.** Button sizes: how many? What heights (44px minimum, but
what about small buttons)?

**53.** Icon-only buttons: circular, square with radius, or just
the icon with padding?

**54.** Toggle/switch style: iOS-style slide, checkbox, or custom?

**55.** Input field style: bordered, filled, underlined? What
background?

**56.** Focus ring: solid outline, dashed, glow, or offset? What
color?

**57.** Should buttons have loading states (spinner replacing text)?

**58.** Link style: underline, accent color, or same as text with
hover underline?

## Companion & personality (59–68)

**59.** Companion size on Today: 80px? 120px? 150px?

**60.** Should the companion have a background shape (circle,
rounded square, blob) or float on the surface?

**61.** Companion "states" to show visually: just idle/quiet, or
also thinking/listening/attention?

**62.** How much personality in copy? "Nothing needs you right now"
vs "Your world is quiet" vs just data?

**63.** Should the sparkle/star mark (✦) appear anywhere besides
the greeting?

**64.** Whimsy level: playful hover effects, micro-interactions,
subtle animations, or completely static?

**65.** Empty states should feel like: warm invitation, honest
absence, or neutral information?

**66.** Error states should feel like: calm explanation, helpful
guidance, or just factual?

**67.** Should the companion ever "react" to state changes (e.g.,
slightly different pose when attention is needed)?

**68.** Voice/tone for system messages: first person ("I noticed…"),
second person ("You have…"), or neutral ("2 items need…")?

## Today screen (69–78)

**69.** Truth sentence position: full-width banner, inline with
greeting, or its own card?

**70.** Health stars: keep them? Replace with a bar? Dots? Or just
the number?

**71.** Summary cards: how many visible at once before scrolling?
2? 3? 4?

**72.** Project attention on Today: individual project cards, one
summary line, or expandable list?

**73.** Journal preview on Today: how many entries? Full text or
truncated? Should it link to Journal?

**74.** Reminders on Today: prominent card or quiet inline list?

**75.** Should Today have a "quick action" area (write journal,
run daily, ask assistant)?

**76.** Companion on Today: large scene presence or small icon in
header?

**77.** The greeting: should time of day matter ("Good morning" vs
"Good evening")?

**78.** Below the summary cards: should there be a "recently
changed" timeline, or just the cards?

**86.** Should "Ask about this" show what context was sent? If so,

**88.** Empty chat state: companion greeting, suggested prompts,
or just the input?
