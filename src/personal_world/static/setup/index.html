Set up Project Worlds

Step 1 of 5: Welcome

Welcome

Project Worlds is a self-hosted home for your projects, journal, tools, and connected services. It keeps track of your projects, journals what happens, and can connect to the services you already run — all on your server. Setup information stays on this server. Services you connect later may receive the requests you choose to make.

This setup takes about five minutes. You will:

  1. let the app prepare itself (automatic — you just watch),
  2. choose how you want to sign in,
  3. pick a couple of comfort settings,
  4. finish, and the app opens.

You do not need any technical knowledge. If something goes wrong, this page tells you what happened and what to try.
